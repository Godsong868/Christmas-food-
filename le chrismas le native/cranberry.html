🎄 Christmas Cranberry Punch 🎄

A refreshing festive cranberry drink with orange, cinnamon and rosemary!

SERVES: 4 | PREP: 10 MIN | COOK: 5 MIN

🍊 Ingredients

  • 500 ml cranberry juice
  • 250 ml orange juice
  • 250 ml sparkling water
  • 1 fresh orange, sliced
  • 100 grams fresh cranberries
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 2 tablespoons honey
  • 2 sprigs of fresh rosemary
  • Ice cubes (optional)

👨‍🍳 Preparation Instructions

  1. Prepare your ingredients.
    Wash the orange, cranberries and rosemary. Slice the orange into thin pieces.
  2. Pour in the cranberry juice.
    Add 500 ml of cranberry juice into a large saucepan.
  3. Add the orange juice.
    Pour in 250 ml of orange juice. Stir for 1 minute.
  4. Add the cinnamon.
    Place 2 cinnamon sticks into the saucepan.
  5. Add the honey.
    Add 2 tablespoons of honey. Stir for 2 minutes until dissolved.
  6. Heat the mixture.
    Warm gently for 5 minutes. Do not allow it to boil.
  7. Let it cool.
    Remove the saucepan from the heat and allow the mixture to cool.
  8. Add the sparkling water.
    Pour in 250 ml of sparkling water and stir gently for 30 seconds.
  9. Decorate your drink.
    Add the orange slices, fresh cranberries and rosemary sprigs.
  10. Serve your Christmas punch!
    Pour into glasses. Add ice cubes if you want a chilled drink.
🎅 Christmas Drink Tip: Add an extra orange slice and a rosemary sprig to each glass for a beautiful Christmas decoration!

✨ Merry Christmas & Happy Sipping! ✨
