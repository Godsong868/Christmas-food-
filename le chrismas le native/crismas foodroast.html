🎄 Christmas Roast Dinner 🎄

A delicious festive roast with crispy potatoes and colourful vegetables!

SERVES: 4 | PREP: 20 MIN | COOK: 1 HR 30 MIN

🥔 Ingredients

For the Roast Chicken

  • 1 whole chicken (about 1.5 kg)
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 teaspoon rosemary
  • 1 teaspoon thyme
  • 3 grams of salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper

For the Roast Potatoes

  • 800 grams of potatoes
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • A pinch of salt

For the Vegetables

  • 3 carrots
  • 200 grams broccoli
  • 150 grams peas
  • 1 tablespoon butter

👨‍🍳 Cooking Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven.
    Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan).
  2. Prepare the chicken.
    Rub the chicken with butter, garlic, rosemary, thyme, salt and pepper.
  3. Roast the chicken.
    Place it in a roasting tray and cook for approximately 1 hour 20 minutes. Check it reaches 75°C internally.
  4. Prepare the potatoes.
    Peel and chop the potatoes. Boil them for 8 minutes, then drain.
  5. Season the potatoes.
    Add 2 tablespoons of oil and a pinch of salt. Stir for 1 minute.
  6. Roast the potatoes.
    Place them on a baking tray and roast for 45–50 minutes. Turn halfway through.
  7. Cook the carrots.
    Boil the carrots for 8–10 minutes until tender.
  8. Add the broccoli.
    Add broccoli to the boiling water for the final 4 minutes.
  9. Add the peas.
    Add peas for the final 2 minutes. Drain all the vegetables.
  10. Add the finishing touch.
    Add 1 tablespoon of butter to the vegetables. Stir for 1 minute.
  11. Serve your Christmas dinner!
    Let the chicken rest for 10–15 minutes. Carve and serve with the golden potatoes and colourful vegetables.
🎅 Christmas Cooking Tip: Turn your potatoes halfway through cooking to make them extra crispy and golden!
