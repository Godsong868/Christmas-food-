🎄 Christmas Cookies 🎄

Delicious homemade Christmas cookies with chocolate chips and festive flavours!

SERVES: 12 COOKIES | PREP: 15 MIN | COOK: 12 MIN

🍪 Ingredients

  • 200 grams plain flour
  • 100 grams softened butter
  • 100 grams brown sugar
  • 50 grams white sugar
  • 1 medium egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • ½ teaspoon baking powder
  • 100 grams chocolate chips
  • 2 grams of salt
  • Optional: Christmas sprinkles

👩‍🍳 Cooking Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven.
    Set your oven to 180°C (160°C fan). Line a baking tray with baking paper.
  2. Mix the butter and sugar.
    Place the softened butter, brown sugar and white sugar into a mixing bowl. Stir for 3 minutes until creamy.
  3. Add the egg.
    Crack in one egg and add the vanilla extract. Mix for 1 minute.
  4. Add the dry ingredients.
    Add the flour, baking powder and 2 grams of salt. Stir gently for 2 minutes.
  5. Add the chocolate chips.
    Pour in 100 grams of chocolate chips. Stir for 1 minute until evenly mixed.
  6. Shape the cookies.
    Roll the mixture into 12 small balls. Place them onto your baking tray, leaving space between each cookie.
  7. Bake the cookies.
    Bake for 10–12 minutes until the edges are lightly golden.
  8. Cool the cookies.
    Leave them on the tray for 5 minutes, then transfer to a cooling rack.
  9. Decorate your cookies.
    Add Christmas sprinkles or melted chocolate once the cookies have cooled.
🎅 Christmas Baking Tip: Don't overbake your cookies! They will continue to firm up as they cool.

✨ Merry Christmas & Happy Baking! ✨
